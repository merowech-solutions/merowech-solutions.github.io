2. No Cookies, No Analytics, Self-Hosted Fonts

We do not set tracking, marketing, or analytics cookies on this website, and we do not embed third-party scripts or external font CDNs (all web fonts such as Manrope and JetBrains Mono are served directly from our own domain). Only if you actively toggle the color theme or language switcher does your browser store that explicit display preference locally in localStorage (`mw-theme`, `mw-lang`), which is strictly required to honor your requested interface setting (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG) and is never transmitted to any server.
