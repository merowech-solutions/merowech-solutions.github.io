Why standard ML pipelines underperform in practice

01

Subtle data leakage, poorly framed target variables, or non-representative validation splits inflate offline metrics and fail silently in production.

02

Teams jump to heavyweight neural or foundation architectures before establishing clean feature representations, uncertainty calibration, and strong statistical baselines.

03

Sequential and time-series applications are treated like independent tabular rows—ignoring temporal dependencies, regime shifts, and horizon uncertainty.
