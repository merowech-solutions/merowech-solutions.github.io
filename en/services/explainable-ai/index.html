Explainable AI (XAI), Model Auditing & Trustworthy ML

We turn opaque machine learning models into transparent, inspectable systems—combining quantitative attribution verification with human-centered visual diagnostics across tabular, deep learning, and time-series applications.

Where black-box models create operational risk

01

Models exploit spurious correlations, data-collection artifacts, or sensor quirks ("Clever-Hans" effects) that collapse silently under real-world distribution shift.

02

Default post-hoc explanations (such as unverified SHAP or saliency maps) are deployed without faithfulness benchmarks—giving engineers and operators false confidence.

03

Regulated workflows and the EU AI Act require intelligible model logic, documented failure boundaries, and effective interfaces for human oversight.
